Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Sonmira (“Sonmira”, “we”, “us”) is a personal singing diary: you sing a short song and Sonmira turns it into a watercolor painting. This policy explains what information the Sonmira iPhone app and the Sonmira web app at sonmira.vercel.app handle, where it is kept, who processes it, and the choices you have.

In short

  • Your diary (recordings, notes, titles, journal entries and paintings) is stored only on your device.
  • When you create a painting, your recording is sent to our server and to OpenAI to understand your words and paint the picture. We do not store your recording.
  • Our server keeps a finished painting and the words it heard for 24 hours, so a painting isn’t lost if the app closes, and then deletes them.
  • We use a random, anonymous identifier instead of your name or email. Sign in with Apple is optional.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, and we do not sell your information.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your diary is stored in the app’s storage on your device. This includes your recordings, any notes, titles and journal entries you write, your paintings, and paintings you have not kept yet (drafts). It is not uploaded to our servers, not backed up by Sonmira and not synced between devices, and signing in does not change that. Deleting the app, or clearing Sonmira’s data in your browser, permanently removes your diary. Sonmira does not currently offer a backup.

The app also stores a few settings on your device, and the iPhone app keeps your anonymous identifier (see section 4) in your iCloud Keychain so it survives a reinstall.

The microphone is used only while you are singing, after you tap to begin. When you choose Save to Photos or Share, the painting or video goes only where you send it; Sonmira asks for permission to add to your photo library and does not read it.

2. Audio recordings and how they are processed

Each time you tap to create a painting:

  • Your recording is sent over an encrypted connection to Sonmira’s server and on to OpenAI, whose speech recognition model writes down any words you sing.
  • The words (if any were heard) are read by an OpenAI language model for their mood and imagery.
  • OpenAI’s image model receives a short text description of what to paint, taken from your words or, when no words were heard, from your note, together with a description of your voice (for example how steady it was, its feeling and its motif) and one of Sonmira’s own reference artworks. Your note is sent only in that case. Your recording itself is not sent to the image model.

Your voice is analyzed on your device to measure qualities such as pitch and steadiness; only the resulting measurements, not the sound, are sent for painting. We do not use your recording to identify you, and Sonmira does not create voiceprints.

Sonmira does not store your recording on its servers. OpenAI processes this information as our service provider under its API terms. According to OpenAI’s API data policies, data sent through its API is not used to train OpenAI’s models by default, and OpenAI may retain it for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse before it is deleted, unless it is required by law to keep it longer.

3. Lyrics and generated paintings

To make sure a painting isn’t lost if the app closes while it is being made, Sonmira’s server keeps, for 24 hours, the words transcribed from your recording, how they were read, the description sent for painting, and the finished painting. After 24 hours they are deleted automatically. Your kept paintings live in your diary on your device.

To vary your paintings, the server also keeps a short history of which of Sonmira’s reference artworks and painting styles were used for your recent paintings. This history contains no words, recordings or images, and expires automatically (the reference history after one year, the style history after 90 days).

4. Anonymous identifiers and accounts

Sonmira does not ask for your name, email address or phone number to use the app. Each installation has a random anonymous identifier. It is used to count paintings, attach purchases to your balance, apply limits and keep the 24-hour results described above.

  • Invitation codes. While Sonmira is invitation-only, the server keeps a coded (hashed) form of the invitation code your device used, linked to your anonymous identifier, for up to one year.
  • Abuse and cost limits. To prevent abuse, the server counts requests per anonymous identifier, per invitation code and per network (IP) address. The counters for your network address expire within about an hour; the others within two days.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional). If you sign in, the server links your anonymous identifier to a keyed, coded (hashed) form of your Apple user identifier, so your balance and purchases can be found again on a new iPhone. We never ask Apple for your name or email. Your diary is not part of your account.

5. Free paintings, subscriptions, purchases and refunds

New users receive a small number of free paintings. Sonmira also offers in-app purchases (such as painting packs) and an auto-renewing subscription. All payments are processed by Apple; Sonmira never receives your payment card or billing details.

To keep your balance accurate, the server keeps a record linked to your anonymous identifier: the free paintings you have used, the paintings and subscription credits you have left, the paid periods of your subscription, and Apple’s transaction identifiers. Apple’s signed transaction information is verified on our server, and Apple may notify our server when a subscription renews, ends or is refunded.

Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies; you can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. When Apple refunds a purchase, the paintings or credits it provided are removed from your balance. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID settings; deleting the app or your Sonmira account does not cancel it.

This balance record is kept while it is in use and expires five years after it last changes, unless you delete your account sooner (see section 8). Apple’s transaction identifiers may be kept for the same period so a purchase cannot be claimed twice.

6. How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

  • create your paintings and deliver them to you;
  • count free paintings, apply purchases and subscriptions, and restore them on a new device;
  • prevent abuse, fraud and unexpected costs, and keep the service working; and
  • answer you when you contact us.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

7. Service providers

We share information only with service providers that help us run Sonmira, and only for the purposes above:

  • OpenAI: speech recognition, reading the words, and painting (see section 2).
  • Vercel: hosts the Sonmira website and servers. Like most hosts, it receives standard request information, such as your IP address and device type, and keeps operational logs.
  • Upstash (through Vercel): the database that holds the server-side records described in this policy.
  • Apple: App Store payments, subscriptions and refunds, and Sign in with Apple.
  • Google Fonts: the app’s typefaces are loaded from Google’s servers, which receive your IP address and browser information when they are loaded.

Our server logs record technical events (for example whether a request succeeded, how long it took, your anonymous identifier or part of it, and purchase transaction identifiers) to keep the service running. They do not contain your recordings, words or paintings. Our providers are based in the United States, so information may be processed there, including for users in Canada. We may also disclose information if required by law.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Your diary. You can delete any entry in the app, and deleting the app removes your whole diary.
  • Delete your account. If you signed in with Apple, you can delete your account in Settings → Account. This revokes Sonmira’s Sign in with Apple access, and removes your account link, your balance record, your invitation place and any stored 24-hour results. You can also choose to erase the diary on that device.
  • Without an account, your server-side information is tied only to a random identifier that we cannot link to your name or email, so we may not be able to find it from an email request. Most of it expires on its own as described above.
  • Privacy requests. Depending on where you live, including several U.S. states and Canada, you may have the right to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Email us at the address below. We will respond as required by applicable law and will not treat you differently for making a request. We may need information to confirm that the request is yours. Canadian residents may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

9. Data security

Information sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). API keys stay on our servers and never reach the app. We store coded (hashed) forms of Apple user identifiers and invitation codes rather than the originals, and access to paid features is protected by signed tokens and limits. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children’s privacy

Sonmira is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will work to delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Sonmira changes. We will post the new version on this page and change the “Last updated” date.
