What these numbers can and cannot say
  • Vocal control measures how sustained and connected the pitch was and how often the sound broke. It picks an artistic register. It is not a singing-quality score.
  • There is no reference melody or beat, so nothing here can judge whether notes or rhythm were correct.
  • Vibrato and slides count as connected. Onset regularity is only a proxy from loudness rises.
  • Emotion and motif come only from the note. With no note: tender and an abstract motif.
  • Thresholds (gathered ≥ 0.50, resolved ≥ 0.78) are artistic calibration, not validated against listeners.
  • Confidence drops with little pitched sound, unclear pitch or mostly noise; low-confidence takes are pulled toward the middle.

Singing test · how the app classifies a whole recording

Nine short takes, each sung a particular way. Before each take, set the tier you expect; after listening back, set the tier you think it deserves. The engine decides exactly as the app does: first whether the words form a complete sung phrase (if not, Loose), then whether the singing is controlled across the whole take (Resolved) or not (Gathered). Your labels are compared with the engine, not treated as correct: the table at the bottom shows where each threshold sits against your voice.
